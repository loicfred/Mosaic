What Mosaic shows

A good-looking total can hide a problem. Mosaic points at it, shows the evidence, and lets you try a change before you make it.

Mosaic compares each product category with the business as a whole, shows where deliveries and reviews are slipping, and lets you try a change in sales to see what it would put under strain. Every figure links back to how it was worked out.

The data is the Olist Brazilian marketplace, January 2017 to August 2018.

Reading the numbers

Every figure carries one of three marks, also shown in the header.

ObservedWhat the data recorded.
Model predictionA forecast or a risk score: a guide, not a promise.
HypotheticalWhat a scenario you chose would mean.

The four data pages

Start on Sales; each page links to the evidence behind what it shows.

SalesThe sales forecast, with tabs for its evidence, an investment suggestion and the possible caveats behind it. DeliveryThe late-delivery rate by month, the customer states where it improved most, and what could hide behind faster delivery. ReviewsThe share of 1 or 2 star reviews, the categories where reviews improved while sales grew, and what could hide behind it. SellersActive sellers a month, the categories where new sellers also find more orders, and what could hide behind a growing seller base.

Running it

Start the website; it starts the Python analytics API itself and stops it when it stops.

  1. Start the website. The data takes a few seconds to load.
  2. For the plain-language summaries, run LM Studio with the model named in config/ai/agents.json.
  3. A local model can take a minute or two. The pages do not wait for it: the suggestion and caveats are asked for only when their button is pressed.

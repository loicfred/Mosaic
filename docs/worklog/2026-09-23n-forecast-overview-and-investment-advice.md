# 2026-09-23 — Forecast-only overview and investment advice when sales rise

Author: Claude

- Added `GET /api/sales/opportunities` (`AI/app/api/get_sales_opportunities.py`, logic in `AI/app/analysis/opportunities.py`). It compares the model's average forecast month with the last 3 observed months. Only when that rises does it list categories whose sales grew at least as fast as the business (with at least BRL 10,000 across both periods). Each category is checked against the business's late-delivery and low-review rates over the last 3 months, needing at least 30 orders for a verdict. It uses `require_model`, so an untrained model returns 503.
- Each candidate carries labels decided in code, with their thresholds in `rules`: `growth_level` (strong ≥ 20 pp, moderate ≥ 5 pp above the business), `size_level` (large ≥ 5 %, medium ≥ 1 % of recent sales), a `level` per rate (better/in_line/worse beyond ±2 pp), and `readiness` (ready/fix_first/unknown). The language model uses these labels as given and never re-judges them.
- On the real data, the forecast is +3.9 %. Health beauty, housewares, pet shop, construction tools and perfumery are growing, and all are in line with the business.
- Java: `MosaicApi.salesOpportunities`, `service/ai/InvestmentAdvisor.java` (the fixed template, plus the `InvestmentAdvisor` chatbot in `config/ai/agents.json`, with every number checked as in `ScenarioNarrator`), `controller/api/InvestmentAdviceController.java` (`/api/overview/advice`, fetched by the page after it loads), and a new assistant tool `MosaicToolbox.investmentOpportunities`.

Removed the hidden-problem statement, the latest-month tiles and the "where to look next" links (`fragments/index/statement.html`, `latest.html`, `next.html`). Those screens are still in the sidebar.
